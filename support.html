Support

How can we help?

Naimap is built and supported by a small, independent team. Here's exactly how to reach us and what to expect.

Ways to get help

Email support

General questions, account-free troubleshooting, anything else — we read every message.

support@naimap.app

Report a bug

Found something broken? Tell us your device, iOS version, and the exact steps to reproduce it.

Report a bug

Request a feature

See something missing? Check the roadmap first, then send it our way.

Frequently asked questions

Most questions about accounts, offline use, exporting, and Pro are already answered.

Browse the FAQ

Known issues

Naimap 1.0 has no publicly known issues at this time. If you find one, it will be listed here in a future update alongside the version it's tracked against — see the changelog.

Response expectations

We aim to reply to support emails within 2 business days. Bug reports with clear reproduction steps are prioritized; feature requests are reviewed regularly and folded into the roadmap when they fit Naimap's focus.
