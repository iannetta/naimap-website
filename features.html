Features

Everything Naimap does — in depth

One thing, done the way a first-party Apple app would.

Free

Infinite Mind Maps

Unlimited maps, unlimited nodes, no artificial ceilings. Drag, pan, and pinch-to-zoom on a canvas built specifically for touch — every gesture feels instant, even on a large, complex map.

Benefits

  • No caps on maps or nodes, ever
  • Smooth, native gesture handling at any zoom level
  • Branch colors keep large maps readable at a glance

What's next

Additional canvas themes are planned — see the roadmap.

Free

Capture Every Detail

Every node can carry long-form notes, an ordered list of links, photos, videos, and documents — so your map becomes where your thinking actually lives, not just a diagram pointing at it elsewhere.

Benefits

  • Rich content attached to the exact idea it belongs to
  • Full undo/redo on every add, edit, and delete
  • On iPad, details open in a side inspector — your canvas never disappears
Free

Auto Arrange

One tap straightens even a sprawling map back into a clean, balanced layout — so organizing your thinking never turns into a second job of tidying boxes.

Benefits

  • Instant, animated re-layout
  • Fully undoable, like every structural change
  • Works identically on a 5-node map or a 50-node one
Free Guided path is Pro

Presentation Mode & Guided Presentation

Present any map full-screen, free-roam. With Naimap Pro, build a custom Presentation Path and walk an audience through it step by step, with private Presenter Notes only you can see.

Benefits

  • Distraction-free, full-screen canvas
  • A curated step order for real talks and lectures
  • Presenter Notes never appear in any export or on the audience's view
PNG & JSON free PDF & Draw.io Pro

Export Anywhere

Every map exports as a PNG image or a JSON backup for free. With Naimap Pro, export a print-ready PDF or a genuine Draw.io (.drawio) file, ready to open in diagrams.net or any compatible tool.

Benefits

  • Full map or just one branch
  • Share sheet, Save to Files, or native printing
  • A real, standards-based Draw.io export — not a lookalike
Free

Built For Productivity

Favorite your most-used maps, search across every node's title and notes, and pick up right where you left off — your library, organized the way you actually work.

Benefits

  • Search across titles and notes, not just map names
  • Favorites and Recents, always one tap away
  • Six starting templates, or a blank canvas

Private by design, on every screen above

No account, no analytics, no network calls — read the full Privacy Policy.
